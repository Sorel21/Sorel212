About

Engineer by training.
Maker by instinct.
Storyteller by choice.

I like work that survives contact with reality: mechanisms that assemble, automation that respects cycle time, prototypes that teach something and documentation that another person can actually use.

The same mindset carries into media. Whether I am modelling a part or framing a shot, I care about structure, clarity and the details that make the result feel intentional.
